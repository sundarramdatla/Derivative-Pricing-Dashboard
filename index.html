Model parameters

Keeps Monte Carlo random draws reproducible. Use the same seed to compare parameter changes, or change it for a new set of paths.
PDE time steps are chosen automatically to satisfy an explicit-scheme stability bound. MC barriers are monitored at discrete steps; PDE barriers are continuously monitored.
Numerical methodology

GBM: S(t+Δt)=S(t)exp[(r−σ²/2)Δt+σ√Δt Z]. Monte Carlo discounts the sample-mean payoff and reports a normal-approximation 95% confidence interval. The explicit PDE marches backward through time, with knockout barriers as absorbing boundaries; knock-ins use vanilla-minus-knockout parity. For non-barrier vanilla/digital contracts, closed-form Black–Scholes acts as a benchmark. The PDE uses a stable time step with a central-space stencil; its outer boundary is truncated.
